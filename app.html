Markup Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works from the costs, percents and prices you type. It does not say what you should charge or whether a price is fair. Fees and taxes count only if you add them to the cost.

The price truth

Your cost and the margin you want (profit as a share of the price).

The swap truth

Markup is profit over cost. Margin is profit over price.

The break-even truth

Sales needed to cover fixed costs.

Markup Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
