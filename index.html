Markup Math

A 50% markup is not a 50% margin. Everybody learns this once, usually on an invoice.

Boundary: arithmetic only Markup Math works from the costs, percents and prices you type. It does not say what you should charge, whether a price is fair, or what fees and taxes apply. Add those costs yourself if you want them counted.

The price truth

Cost $12.00 at a 40% margin: sell at $20.00, profit $8.00 (that is a 66.7% markup).

The swap truth

A 50% markup is a 33.3% margin. A 40% margin is a 66.7% markup. Same money, different base.

The break-even truth

Fixed costs $300.00, price $20.00, unit cost $12.00: 38 sales to cover fixed costs.
